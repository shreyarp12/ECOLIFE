About EcoLife

EcoLife is a Local Eco-Awareness project designed to increase environmental awareness among students and members of the local community.

The website provides short environmental quizzes, useful eco-friendly tips, daily environmental challenges, and a feedback system.

🎯 Project Objective

To educate people about recycling, conservation, pollution reduction and simple sustainable habits through an interactive website.

📋 Fieldwork

A paper-based survey can be conducted with 20 or more students or neighbors to understand their environmental knowledge.
