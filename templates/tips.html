💡 Eco-Friendly Tips

Small habits can create a big difference.

1

♻️ Recycle More

Separate paper, plastic, glass and metal waste. Recycling helps reduce pollution and saves valuable natural resources.

2

💧 Save Water

Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth. Fix leaking taps and avoid wasting water during daily activities.

3

🌱 Plant Trees

Plant and protect trees because they provide oxygen, reduce pollution and create a healthier environment for people and animals.

4

💡 Save Electricity

Switch off lights, fans and electronic devices when they are not being used. Use LED bulbs to save energy.

5

🛍️ Avoid Plastic

Use reusable shopping bags, bottles and containers instead of single-use plastic products.

6

🚲 Use Green Transport

Walk, cycle or use public transport whenever possible. This helps reduce air pollution and carbon emissions.

7

🥗 Reduce Food Waste

Take only the food you need and store leftovers properly. Compost food scraps whenever possible.

8

🌍 Keep Your Surroundings Clean

Never throw waste on roads, parks or other public places. Always use the correct dustbin and encourage others to keep the environment clean.

🌍 Every Small Action Matters!

Start with one eco-friendly habit today and encourage others to do the same.
